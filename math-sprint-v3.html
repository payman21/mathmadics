How scoring works

  • Correct answers score points, plus speed and streak bonuses
  • Wrong answers cost points; passing costs 4 seconds
  • Difficulty increases with each question
Base score Question level × difficulty multiplier

Easy starts at Level 1, Medium at Level 3, and Hard at Level 5. Harder levels also pay more per answer — Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2.2 — so every difficulty competes on the same leaderboard.

Practice lets you play a 30-second, 1-minute, 2-minute or 5-minute round at any difficulty. Practice rounds are never saved to your high scores or the global leaderboard, so use them to warm up freely. Only 2-minute solo rounds started with Play or Daily challenge are ranked.
